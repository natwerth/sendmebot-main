Quick setup:

  • Add recipients in Tracker.
  • Create emails with → New template.
  • Add approved senders and signatures in Senders.
  • Add reusable image names and Drive links in the image table.
  • Use → Email selected now or Schedule selected.

Manual variables:

If editing directly in the sheet, wrap variables in double brackets.

{{Name}}
{{Sender Signature}}
{{ET Header}}
